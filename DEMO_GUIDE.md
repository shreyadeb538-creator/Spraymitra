# Demo guide for judges (about 4 minutes)

1. **Problem (30 s)**: supervisors cannot see how, or whether, each row was sprayed. Show Live field.
2. **Live map (45 s)**: point at the serpentine map, numbered GPS dots and the KPI strip. Say the data is simulated.
3. **Fault injection (60 s)**: click a sprayer card, open *Sprayer kit*, toggle **Low pressure**. Return to *Live field*: the alert appears, the card flags the fault, the score drops, thin cells turn red. Click **Acknowledge**.
4. **Second fault (30 s)**: toggle **Rushing** on another sprayer to show pace-based thin coverage.
5. **Team & rewards (30 s)**: leaderboard, badges, sample eligibility rule (score ≥ 85 and no skipped rows).
6. **Audit (45 s)**: *Coverage audit*, then **Generate audit summary** at round end: verified rows vs re-spray rows and a ready/not-ready statement.
7. **Close (20 s)**: retrofit kit diagram in *Sprayer kit*, then the roadmap: bench build, field pilot with TRA.

Tips: use 16× speed; use Restart round for a second run. Be upfront: the 15–25% pesticide saving is the challenge target, not a measured result. Likely questions: GPS accuracy under canopy (needs field testing), kit cost (needs vendor quotes), validation of thresholds (needs TRA agronomists).
